Privacy Policy

Texas Land Records Tools -- Chrome Extension

Texas Land Records Tools does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The extension does not track browsing activity, and no data is sent to external servers.

All processing performed by the extension happens locally in your browser. No account or login is required, and no cookies or analytics are used.

The extension does not access any data beyond what is necessary to provide its functionality on supported county and title research websites.

This policy may be updated if the extension's functionality changes. Any updates will be reflected on this page.
